## What changed

**Controls.** `ControlPosition` (bottom / leading / trailing) replaced the rail
and continuous position; `ControlClusterLayout` is the pure geometry (cluster
size 286×88, the three centres, grip centre, slot rects, slot hit-testing, and
the control order). A 44 pt three-dot grip at the end **opposite Undo** drags a
puck over phantom slots; the cluster only moves when a slot is released. The
photo never resizes or shifts for chrome. `showButtons` hides the cluster; swipe
is always available. `undoSide` (default `leading`) puts Undo at either outer
end, with Trash and Keep adjacent in the swipe wells' order. See ADR-0006,
0007, 0009, 0011.

**Entry / Choose a photo / Settings.** Wordmark, one circular `Start here`,
`Resume`, and a `Review · N` chip. `ChoosePhotoView` (formerly Start Here) owns
`Newest` and `Random`. Settings leads with inline read-only statistics, then
`Show buttons`, the three positions, the Undo side and a reset. The preset list,
`Tap to keep` and the separate statistics screen are gone.

## Spawning the next window

Fresh context windows are `pi --print`, run from the repository:

```sh
pi -p --no-session --provider command-code --model deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash \
  "Read docs/handoffs/2026-09-23-redesign-10-12.md, then <task>"
```

`--tools read,bash,ls,find,grep` makes a window read-only (used for the two
reviews). `--mode json` gives JSONL instead of text; `--fork <id>` branches a
session. Spawned windows share the working tree: parallelise read-only work,
serialise writers.
