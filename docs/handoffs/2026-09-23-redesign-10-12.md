## What changed

**`SWIPRKit/ControlPreferences.swift`** — rewritten. `ControlPreset`,
`ControlRail` and the continuous `position: Double` are gone. `ControlPosition`
(bottom/leading/trailing) and `ControlPreferences { position, showButtons,
defaultDirection }` replace them, with tolerant decoding of the old `rail` /
`position` / `preset` keys. `ControlClusterLayout` is the new pure geometry
(cluster size, the three centres, grip centre, slot rects and slot hit-testing).

**`SWIPR/Views/ViewerView.swift`** — the tray no longer moves. A 44 pt three-dot
grip in the tray's leading end carries a plain `DragGesture`; it lifts a puck
that follows the finger, draws phantom slots, and moves the cluster only when
released over a slot. The photo lane reservation and `photoLaneOffset` are
deleted, so the fitted frame is identical at every position. `Show buttons`
hides the cluster and grip while swipe stays enabled. Swipe is no longer
preset-gated.

**`SWIPR/Views/EntryView.swift`** — wordmark, one circular `Start here` action,
`Resume` only while a session waits, a trailing `Review · N` chip only while
marks wait, and a disabled neutral empty state reading "No photos to sort." No
footer.

**`SWIPR/Views/ChoosePhotoView.swift`** — new (replaces `StartHereGridView`).
Title "Choose a photo", identifiers `choosePhoto.*`, route `.choosePhoto`, with
`Newest` and `Random` above the grid. At accessibility sizes the chrome scrolls
and the buttons stack so labels are not truncated.

**`SWIPR/Views/SettingsView.swift`** — inline read-only statistics at the very
top, a `Show buttons` toggle, the three position choices and a reset. The preset
list, `Tap to keep` and the statistics row are gone. `StatisticsView.swift` was
deleted with the `.statistics` route.

**`SWIPR/Views/TutorialView.swift`** — no preset branch; always teaches the
drag, the buttons as an alternative, and dragging the three dots.

**`SWIPR/ViewModels/AppModel.swift`** — `startRecent` → `startNewest`,
`showStartHere` → `showChoosePhoto`, `startHere(assetID:)` → `startFrom(assetID:)`,
route `.startHere` → `.choosePhoto`, `.statistics` removed.

**Tests** — `ControlPreferencesTests` rewritten and `ControlClusterLayoutTests`
added; `SWIPRUITests` and `PlaySessionUITests` rewritten for fixed positions,
the grip drag, the new entry screen and Choose a photo. App tests only renamed.

**Docs** — `docs/SPEC.md` §2/§3/§4/§4a/§7, `docs/VISION.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`,
`README.md` and `docs/TESTING.md` updated to the new behaviour;
`docs/screenshots/` refreshed from the green run; `docs/ux-review.md` added.

## Traps carried forward

- Inside a restricted sandbox, `xcodebuild` needs
  `OTHER_SWIFT_FLAGS='$(inherited) -Xfrontend -disable-sandbox'` and
  `-derivedDataPath ./.derivedData`, and device runs need full file access.
- `-only-testing` needs the three-part form: a target-only form
  (`-only-testing:SWIPRUITests`) works; the two-part target/class form silently
  runs 0 tests.
- UI-test screenshots live in the `.xcresult`; export with
  `xcrun xcresulttool export attachments` and read the `manifest.json` to recover
  attachment names. The drag helpers remain position-sensitive, so `dragGrip`
  now targets screen points.
- `Settings` statistics rows and the Choose a photo chrome switch layout at
  accessibility sizes on purpose — do not "simplify" them back to one `HStack`
  without checking the AX5 screenshots.

## Open decisions for the owner

Recorded in full in [`docs/ux-review.md`](../ux-review.md). In short:

1. Centre the three buttons rather than the tray at the bottom position (the
   grip currently pushes them ~29 pt right of centre). Recommended.
2. Raise the idle fade from 55% to about 70%.
3. Say "0 bytes" rather than "≈ 0 bytes" when nothing has been deleted.
4. A marks-only entry state is not reachable in the current product; if the
   owner wants it, that is a `PersistedSession.isResumable` rule change.
